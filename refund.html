Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: December 20, 2025

CampusKart is a student-only classifieds platform. We charge fees exclusively for digital services such as Premium Ad Hosting and Featured Listings. We do not facilitate, process, or receive payments for any physical goods listed on the platform.

1. No Refunds for Premium Services

Once a Premium Ad is activated or any platform service fee is paid, the digital service is considered instantly consumed. Therefore:

  • No refunds will be issued for any paid Premium Ad Hosting, Featured Listings, or other platform services.
  • This policy applies regardless of whether the ad receives responses or achieves the desired outcome.

2. Cancellation of Ads

Users may delete or remove their ads at any time through the app. However:

  • No pro-rata or partial refund will be provided for the remaining duration of a paid Premium Ad.
  • Deletion of an ad does not entitle the user to any refund.

3. Transactions for Physical Goods

Important: CampusKart is not responsible for any refunds, returns, or disputes related to physical items (books, drafters, electronics, etc.) traded between students. All such transactions are direct peer-to-peer agreements between the buyer and seller. CampusKart bears no liability for the quality, safety, or delivery of physical goods.
