PRIVACY POLICY

Last updated December 20, 2025

This Privacy Policy describes how CampusKart - Everything College ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and shares personal information when you use our mobile application and services ("Services").

CampusKart provides a platform for college students to post classified ads and discover campus announcements. We do not facilitate transactions or delivery — it is a peer-to-peer advertising service. All deals for physical items are completed offline between users.

If you have questions about this policy, please contact us at visheshaggarwal1708@gmail.com.

Summary of Key Points

  • What personal information do we collect? Primarily your email address (used for account creation and login). We also collect payment details only when you purchase premium features.
  • Do we collect sensitive information? No.
  • Do we share your information with third parties? Only with payment processors (Razorpay) for premium features and with other users when you post ads or communicate publicly on the platform.
  • How long do we keep your data? As long as your account is active, or as needed for legal requirements.
  • Your rights: You can access, update, or delete your account at any time via the app settings or by contacting us.

1. What Information Do We Collect?

Information you provide to us

We collect the following personal information when you create an account or use our Services:

  • Email address (required for registration and login)

Payment Data

If you purchase premium ad features (e.g., featured listings), we collect payment information necessary to process the transaction. All payment data is securely handled and stored by our payment processor Razorpay. You can view their privacy policy here: https://razorpay.com/privacy/.

Important clarification about payments:
CampusKart is a classifieds platform. We only collect payments for premium services such as featured ads or platform fees. We do not collect or process payments for any physical goods or services listed by users. All transactions for items (books, electronics, etc.) happen directly between buyers and sellers offline.

2. How Do We Use Your Information?

We use your personal information to:

  • Create and manage your account
  • Enable communication between users (e.g., when responding to ads)
  • Process payments for premium features
  • Improve and maintain the Services
  • Comply with legal obligations

3. When Do We Share Your Information?

We share your information only in these limited cases:

  • With Razorpay to process payments for premium features
  • With other users when you post ads, announcements, or communicate publicly on the platform (your posted content and profile are visible to others)
  • In connection with a business transfer (e.g., merger or acquisition)
  • When required by law

4. Do We Use Cookies and Tracking Technologies?

Yes, we use cookies and similar technologies (including Google Analytics) to improve app performance, ensure security, and analyze usage. You can control cookies through your device settings. For more on Google’s practices, see their privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy.

5. How Long Do We Keep Your Information?

We keep your personal information only as long as your account is active or as required for legal, tax, or accounting purposes. When you delete your account, we remove your data from our active systems.

6. How Do We Keep Your Information Safe?

We use industry-standard technical and organizational security measures to protect your data. However, no system is 100% secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Do We Collect Information from Minors?

No. Our Services are intended for users 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

8. What Are Your Privacy Rights?

You can:

  • Access or update your information in your account settings
  • Delete your account at any time via the app or by emailing us
  • Contact us with any privacy-related requests

We will respond to legitimate requests promptly and in accordance with applicable law.

9. Do-Not-Track Features

Currently, we do not respond to Do-Not-Track browser signals, as there is no uniform standard.

10. Do We Update This Policy?

Yes. We will notify you of material changes by posting the updated policy with a new "Last updated" date. Please review it periodically.
